Privacy Policy

Short version: Sharper collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Effective September 26, 2026. This policy covers the Sharper: Daily Lessons app for iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Sharper has no servers of its own and no accounts. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and it doesn't share or sell data with anyone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your preferences, the categories you create, your lessons, bookmarks, streak and reminder time are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also clear them in Sharper's Settings.

Wikipedia

To find and write new lessons, Sharper fetches public articles from Wikipedia's API. Those requests go directly from your iPhone to the Wikimedia Foundation and contain only the article or search being requested, such as a topic you typed into Teach me about…. Like any website, Wikimedia receives your device's IP address when you connect; its handling is described in the Wikimedia privacy policy. Opening a “Go deeper” link opens Wikipedia in your browser.

On-device intelligence

Lessons and answers to follow-up questions are written by Apple's on-device model where your iPhone supports it. That processing happens on your iPhone; Sharper doesn't send your questions anywhere.

Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push service is involved.

Emailing us

If you choose to email us, for example with Report a problem, we receive what you send, including your email address and the lesson details the app fills in. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem, and we don't add you to any list.

Children

Sharper collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
