Get in touch

Found a mistake in a lesson, hit a bug, or have an idea? Email mrcocotoasty@gmail.com. In the app, Report a problem at the end of any lesson fills in the details for you.

Common questions

Where do lessons come from? A set of hand-written lessons ships with the app. New ones are written on your iPhone from Wikipedia articles, using Apple's on-device intelligence where your iPhone supports it. Each lesson links to its sources.

Can I learn about something outside Math, Science and History? Yes. Add your own category in Settings, or use Teach me about… in the Library to get a lesson on any topic.

Why didn't I get a reminder? Check that Daily reminder is on in Sharper's Settings and that notifications for Sharper are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.

Does Sharper need an account? No. There's no sign-up, and everything stays on your iPhone.
